title: Storyboard
---

## Act 1 · Chaos (0–7 s)

Requirements are scattered across chat, email, the wiki, screenshots, and PDFs. The fragments spin faster and faster as the screen reads: **"One spec, scattered across twelve places."**

## Act 2 · Collapse (7–12 s)

Every fragment is pulled into a single point and compressed into one file: `spec.taco.html`.

## Act 3 · Reveal (12–17 s)

Three bubbles (green, blue, orange) pop in and form the Taco logo.

## Act 4 · The real product (17–33 s)

The camera pushes into the real Taco: file tree, outline, WYSIWYG editing, anchored comments.

## Act 5 · The loop (33–41 s)

The file travels between human and agent: pack → review → Handoff → sync.

## Act 6 · Call to action (41–50 s)

The install command types itself out, and the film lands on the logo and tagline.
